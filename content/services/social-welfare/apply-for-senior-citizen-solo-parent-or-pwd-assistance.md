# Apply for Senior Citizen, Solo Parent, or PWD Assistance

How to get a Senior Citizens ID, a Solo Parent ID or a PWD ID from the municipal social welfare office (MSWDO).

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO):** (044) 760-7029. Municipal grounds.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MSWDO-CITIZENS-CHARTER.pdf) gives its hours as Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, with no noon break. The document is marked 2019, 1st edition, so ask the office if anything has changed.

## Senior Citizens ID card

The MSWDO reviews your papers and interviews you. The ID card is then prepared and signed by the Municipal Mayor and the head of the Office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA).

- **Who can apply:** Senior citizens who live in Paombong. The document prints the age line unclearly.
- **Fee:** None. No price is printed for the purchase booklet that the document mentions.
- **Processing time:** Step times of 5 to 10 minutes are listed, with no total.

### What to bring

- **Certificate from the Punong Barangay (1 original).** Where to get it: Your barangay captain's office.
- **Birth certificate or baptismal certificate (1 photocopy, if needed).** Where to get it: Local Civil Registrar.
- **Accomplished application form.** Where to get it: Office of Senior Citizens Affairs.
- **Two 1x1 pictures.** You provide these.
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Bring the requirements to the MSWDO. The office reviews them and interviews you.
2. Fill out the intake sheet.
3. Wait while the ID card is prepared and signed, then receive it. The document also names a purchase booklet at this step.

## Solo Parent ID card

For solo parents who meet the requirements. The ID card is signed by the Municipal Mayor and the MSWDO.

- **Who can apply:** Paombong residents who qualify as solo parents.
- **Fee:** None. No price is printed for the purchase booklet that the document mentions.
- **Processing time:** 10 minutes each is listed for signing and for issuing the ID card, with no total.

### What to bring

- **Certificate from the Punong Barangay (1 original), if separated or abandoned for more than a year.** Where to get it: Your barangay captain's office.
- **Birth certificate of each child below 22 years old (1 photocopy each).** Where to get it: Local Civil Registrar.
- **Death certificate of the deceased spouse.** Where to get it: Local Civil Registrar.
- **Accomplished application form.** Where to get it: MSWDO.
- **Affidavit of Undertaking, notarized.** Where to get it: Signed by the Barangay Captain.
- **Joint affidavit of two disinterested persons, notarized.** Where to get it: Your two witnesses.
- **Two 1x1 pictures.** You provide these.
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Bring the requirements to the MSWDO, which reviews them.
2. Fill out the intake sheet.
3. Wait while the ID card is prepared and signed, then receive it. The document also names a purchase booklet at this step.

## PWD ID card

For persons with disability. The ID card is signed by the Municipal Mayor and the MSWDO.

- **Who can apply:** Persons with disability who live in Paombong.
- **Fee:** None. No price is printed for the purchase booklet that the document mentions.
- **Processing time:** About 28 minutes, adding up the times listed for each step.

### What to bring

- **Certificate from the Punong Barangay (1 original).** Where to get it: Your barangay captain's office.
- **Birth certificate (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: Local Civil Registrar.
- **Medical certificate that states the specific type of disability.** Where to get it: A specialist or appropriate physician.
- **Two 1x1 pictures.** You provide these.
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Bring the requirements to the MSWDO, which reviews them.
2. Fill out the intake sheet.
3. Wait while the ID card is prepared and signed, then receive it. The document also names a purchase booklet at this step.
