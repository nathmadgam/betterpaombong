# Apply for Senior Citizen, Solo Parent, or PWD Assistance

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO):** (044) 760-7029. Municipal grounds.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MSWDO-CITIZENS-CHARTER.pdf) lists the Senior Citizens ID card and purchase booklet, the Solo Parent ID card and the Person with Disability (PWD) ID card. The document gives the requirements and steps for each.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).

Know how this works in Paombong? [Help write this page](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong).

_Sources: [Municipality of Paombong, Contact Us page](https://paombong.gov.ph/contact-us/), [Citizen's Charter of the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MSWDO-CITIZENS-CHARTER.pdf).
