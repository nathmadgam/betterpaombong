# Get Family and Community Welfare Support

Help for families in difficulty from the municipal social welfare office (MSWDO): crisis assistance, referrals, certificates, and protection for women and children.

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO):** (044) 760-7029. Municipal grounds.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MSWDO-CITIZENS-CHARTER.pdf) gives its hours as Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, with no noon break. The document is marked 2019, 1st edition, so ask the office if anything has changed.

## Assistance to individuals in crisis

Cash assistance for residents in a crisis. The MSWDO interviews you, reviews your papers and prepares a Certificate of Eligibility before the cash is released.

- **Who can apply:** Residents of Paombong.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 2 to 8 minutes for the interview and review, and 2 minutes to prepare and sign the Certificate of Eligibility, with no total.

### What to bring

- **Barangay certificate of indigency (1 original).** Where to get it: Your barangay captain's office.
- **Medical certificate or medical abstract (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The hospital where the patient is confined.
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Bring the requirements to the MSWDO. The office interviews you and reviews them.
2. Fill out the intake sheet. The office prepares and signs the Certificate of Eligibility.
3. Receive the cash assistance.

## Referral letter to another agency

The MSWDO prepares, signs and releases a referral letter.

- **Who can apply:** Residents of Paombong who need a referral.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 2 to 8 minutes for the review, then 5 minutes each to prepare and to release the letter.

### What to bring

- **Barangay certificate of indigency (1 original).** Where to get it: Your barangay captain's office.
- **Medical certificate or medical abstract (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The hospital where the patient is confined.
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Bring the requirements to the MSWDO, which reviews them.
2. Fill out the intake sheet and answer the interview.
3. Receive the signed referral letter.

## PhilHealth certificate for indigent families

The MSWDO issues a PhilHealth certification for indigent families and individuals, to be used for medical services at PhilHealth-accredited hospitals.

- **Who can apply:** Indigent residents of Paombong.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** About 12 minutes, adding up the times listed for each step.

### What to bring

- **Barangay certificate of indigency (1 original).** Where to get it: Your barangay captain's office.
- **Medical certificate or medical abstract (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The hospital where the patient is confined.
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Bring the requirements to the MSWDO, which reviews them.
2. Fill out the intake sheet and answer the interview.
3. Receive the signed PhilHealth certificate.

## Certificate of indigency

The MSWDO prepares, signs and releases a certificate of indigency.

- **Who can apply:** Residents of Paombong of any age.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** About 16 minutes, adding up the times listed for each step.

### What to bring

- **Certificate of indigency and residency (1 original).** Where to get it: Your barangay captain's office.
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Bring the requirements to the MSWDO, which reviews them.
2. Fill out the intake sheet and answer the interview.
3. Receive the signed certificate of indigency.

## Social case study report

A report prepared by a social worker after an in-depth interview and a home visit.

- **Who can apply:** Residents of Paombong aged 18 and above.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 5 minutes for the review, 10 minutes for the in-depth interview and 1 day for the home visit are listed. Preparing the report is listed at 15 minutes for medical cases and 3 days for court-related cases, and releasing it at 5 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Certificate of indigency (1 original).** Where to get it: Your barangay captain's office.
- **Request letter from the agency concerned (optional).**
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Bring the requirements to the MSWDO, which reviews them.
2. Fill out the intake sheet and take part in the in-depth interview.
3. Expect a home visit by the social worker.
4. Receive the signed social case study report.

## Care and protection for women and children

The MSWDO helps women and children who are victims of abuse, violence, abandonment or neglect. The document lists rescue, counseling and family reconciliation, help with the medico-legal examination and the police report, help in filing a case, and referral for temporary shelter.

- **Who can apply:** Battered or maltreated women and children, people who are emotionally disturbed, victims of rape, sexual abuse or involuntary prostitution, and concerned citizens.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 5 to 10 minutes is listed for each step, and the document says it depends on the case.

### What to bring

- **Referral letter for a medico-legal examination.** Where to get it: Philippine National Police.
- **Medico-legal certificate.** Where to get it: An accredited hospital.
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Go to the MSWDO and tell the social worker what happened.
2. Fill out the intake sheet so the office can decide what help is needed.
3. The social worker and the police assist with the medico-legal examination, with filing a case and with referrals to other agencies.
4. Attend the counseling sessions. The social worker may also visit the home.

If someone is in immediate danger, call **911**, the [national emergency hotline](https://bettergov.ph/philippines/hotlines).
