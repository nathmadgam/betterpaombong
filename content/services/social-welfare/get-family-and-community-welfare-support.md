# Get Family and Community Welfare Support

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO):** (044) 760-7029. Municipal grounds.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MSWDO-CITIZENS-CHARTER.pdf) lists assistance to individuals in crisis situations, case referrals, the certificate of indigency, the social case study report, and care and protection for children and women.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).

Know how this works in Paombong? [Help write this page](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong).

_Sources: [Municipality of Paombong, Contact Us page](https://paombong.gov.ph/contact-us/), [Citizen's Charter of the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MSWDO-CITIZENS-CHARTER.pdf). The Citizen's Charter was read on 24 November 2026. Spotted an error? [Open an issue](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong/issues)._
