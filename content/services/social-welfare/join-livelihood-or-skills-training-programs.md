# Join Livelihood or Skills Training Programs

Where to ask about livelihood and skills training, and how the employment office (PESO) helps residents find work.

---

## Where to go

- **Public Employment Service Office (PESO):** 0949-994-9587. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CITIZENCHARTER_PESO_2025_final.pdf) names skills development and training, and livelihood and emergency employment programs such as TUPAD, among its functions. It gives no requirements or steps for them, so please ask the office.

## Job referral (employment facilitation)

The PESO gives Paombong residents access to jobs through job matching, referrals and placement with local and national employers.

- **Who can apply:** All residents of Paombong.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 15 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Accomplished request slip.** Where to get it: PESO.
- **Resume.** You provide this.
- **Valid ID.** You provide this.
- **If the company asks for them: barangay clearance, NBI clearance, police clearance, birth certificate, transcript of records or diploma.** You provide these.

### Steps

1. Ask at the PESO information desk.
2. Fill out the request slip and the PESO Employment Information System form.
3. Register in the logbook.
4. Receive the referral letter and go to the company to apply for the job.

## Government Internship Program

The program gives young workers work experience in the projects and programs of government agencies.

- **Who can apply:** People aged 18 to 30 who finished high school, senior high school, its Alternative Learning System equivalent or a technical-vocational course, and who have no work experience. The document lists exceptions for people affected by disasters, armed conflict or displacement.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 2 hours and 23 minutes.

### What to bring

- **GIP application form.** Where to get it: PESO.
- **Birth certificate.** Where to get it: Philippine Statistics Authority.
- **Transcript of records, Form 137 or 138, diploma, certificate of graduation or a school certification.** Where to get it: The school where you studied.
- **Certificate of indigency.** Where to get it: The barangay where you live.
- **Updated resume.** You provide this.
- **2x2 picture with white background, name and signature, and a valid ID.** You provide these.

### Steps

1. Register and submit your documents to the PESO, then wait for its advice on the interview schedule.
2. Fill out the GIP application form.
3. Submit the required documents and the accomplished application form.
4. Attend the orientation.
