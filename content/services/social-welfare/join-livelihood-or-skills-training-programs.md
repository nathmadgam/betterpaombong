# Join Livelihood or Skills Training Programs

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Public Employment Service Office (PESO):** 0949-994-9587. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CITIZENCHARTER_PESO_2025_final.pdf) names skills development and training, and livelihood and emergency employment programs such as TUPAD, among its functions.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).

Know how this works in Paombong? [Help write this page](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong).

_Sources: [Municipality of Paombong, Contact Us page](https://paombong.gov.ph/contact-us/), [Citizen's Charter of the Public Employment Service Office](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CITIZENCHARTER_PESO_2025_final.pdf). The Citizen's Charter was read on 24 November 2026. Spotted an error? [Open an issue](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong/issues)._
