# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters

Where to ask for relief goods and other help after a typhoon, flood, fire or other calamity.

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO):** (044) 760-7029. Municipal grounds.
- **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO):** (044) 761-3199. MDRRMO Building, Poblacion.

The MSWDO's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MSWDO-CITIZENS-CHARTER.pdf) gives its hours as Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, with no noon break. That document is marked 2019, 1st edition, so ask the office if anything has changed.

## Disaster relief assistance from the MSWDO

For individuals and families who are victims of natural or man-made calamities such as typhoons, floods and fire. The document lists relief goods, free food delivered to evacuation centers, limited housing materials and counseling.

- **Who can apply:** Residents of Paombong who are victims of a natural or man-made calamity.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 3 minutes and 10 minutes are listed for the steps, with no total.

### What to bring

- **Report from the barangay or certificate from the Punong Barangay (1 original).** Where to get it: Your barangay captain's office.
- **Photocopy of one government-issued ID, such as a PhilHealth ID, driver's license, passport, voter's ID or certificate, UMID or PhilSys ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Bring the requirements to the MSWDO, which reviews them.
2. Fill out the intake sheet and answer the interview.
3. The office assesses whether you qualify for relief assistance.

## Financial or livelihood assistance from the MDRRMO

Assistance for families and individuals affected by a tropical cyclone, a fire or a similar incident. The steps in the document are for Emergency Shelter Assistance (ESA).

- **Who can apply:** Individuals affected by a disaster.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 2 hours and 52 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Request letter addressed to the Municipal Mayor (2 copies: 1 original and 1 photocopy).** You write this.
- **Barangay certification (1 copy).** Where to get it: Your barangay.
- **Narrative report from the victim, with photos.** You write this.
- **Supporting documents: residency, ownership of the property, valid damage report.** Where to get it: Your barangay.

### Steps

1. Submit the requirements.
2. Wait for the office to tell you that the ESA voucher is available.
3. Claim the assistance at the Municipal Treasurer's Office, where you sign for the voucher check.

The document addresses the letter to the Local Chief Executive, which in a municipality is the Mayor.
