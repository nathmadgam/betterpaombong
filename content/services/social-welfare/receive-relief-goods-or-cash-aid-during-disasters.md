# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the offices to ask are:

- **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO):** (044) 760-7029. Municipal grounds.
- **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO):** (044) 761-3199. MDRRMO Building, Poblacion.

The MSWDO's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MSWDO-CITIZENS-CHARTER.pdf) lists disaster relief assistance and assistance to individuals in crisis situations. The MDRRMO's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MDRRMO-Arta.pdf) lists financial and livelihood assistance for victims of a disaster.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).
