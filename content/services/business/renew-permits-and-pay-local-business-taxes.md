# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the offices to ask are:

- **Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO):** 0918-938-1967. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

The BPLO's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/BPLO-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists the renewal of a business permit. The Treasurer's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Treasurer_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists the collection of business tax.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).
