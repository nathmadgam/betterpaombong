# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes

How to renew a business permit, pay the business tax and close a business.

---

## Where to go

- **Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO):** 0918-938-1967. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

## Renew a business permit

A business permit is required for all investors and business owners who operate a business in Paombong. It is renewed at the BPLO.

- **Who can apply:** Owners of an existing business.
- **Fee:** No fixed amount is printed. The tax and fees are assessed for each business under Municipal Ordinance 2019-02-P-34 and Panlalawigang Ordinansa Blg. C-005. One endorsing office charges under the Local Revenue Code, and the fire safety fee follows the Fire Code (Republic Act No. 9514).
- **Processing time:** 3 working days, 2 hours and 5 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Unified Application Form (1 original).** Where to get it: BPLO.
- **DTI, SEC or CDA registration (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: DTI, SEC or CDA.
- **Barangay business clearance (1 original).** Where to get it: Barangay hall where the business is located.
- **Notarized contract of lease (1 original), if the place is leased.** Where to get it: The lessor.
- **Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (1 original).** Where to get it: Bureau of Fire Protection.
- **Sanitary permit (1 original).** Where to get it: Municipal Health Office or Rural Health Unit.
- **Occupancy permit (1 original).** Where to get it: Municipal Engineering Office.
- **Community Tax Certificate or cedula (original or photocopy).** Where to get it: Municipal Treasurer's Office.
- **Sworn statement of receipts, financial statement signed by the accountant, or book of sales or receipts, if any (1 original).** Where to get it: Accounting firm.
- **Income Tax Return (1 original).** Where to get it: Bureau of Internal Revenue.
- **Market clearance (1 original), for market stall holders.** Where to get it: Office of the Market Master.
- **Receipt for the Certificate of Non-Coverage (1 original).** Where to get it: Provincial Capitol.
- **Real property tax receipt (amilyar) for the zoning approval (original or photocopy).** Where to get it: Municipal Treasurer's Office.
- **Authorization or special power of attorney (1 original), if the owner cannot process the papers.** Where to get it: The business owner.
- **Government-issued ID (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Submit the accomplished and signed Unified Application Form and the requirements to the BPLO, and wait for the assessment.
2. Go to the endorsing offices the BPLO sends you to: the Engineering Office, the MPDO, the Health Office, the MENRO and the Bureau of Fire Protection.
3. Pay the assessed tax and fees at the Municipal Treasurer's Office and get the official receipt.
4. Return to the BPLO with the receipt. The permit is released within 1 to 3 days.
5. Sign the receiving logbook and receive the business permit, with the business plate or sticker.

The document also lists renewals filed during the Business One Stop Shop, with a total time of 2 hours and 10 minutes. It does not say when the One Stop Shop runs.

## Pay the business tax

Business owners pay their business taxes and fees at the Office of the Municipal Treasurer.

- **Who can apply:** All business owners in the municipality.
- **Fee:** The amount shown in your Tax Order of Payment.
- **Processing time:** 12 minutes per transaction.

### What to bring

- **Tax Order of Payment (1 copy).** Where to get it: Business Permit and Licensing Office.

### Steps

1. Present a copy of the Tax Order of Payment.
2. Pay the amount shown in it.
3. Get the official receipt.

## Close a business

This is the application for the closure, also called retirement, of a business in Paombong. The BPLO issues a Business Closure Certificate.

- **Who can apply:** Owners of a business that is closing.
- **Fee:** An assessed amount under Municipal Ordinance 2019-02-P-34. No amount is printed.
- **Processing time:** 57 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Barangay business closure certificate (1 original).** Where to get it: Barangay hall where the business is located.
- **Affidavit for business closure (1 original).** Where to get it: A law firm or notary public.
- **Sworn statement of gross sales or receipts (1 original).** Where to get it: Accounting firm.
- **Previous Mayor's Permit.** You provide this.
- **Special power of attorney or authorization letter (1 original), if the owner cannot process the closure.** Where to get it: The business owner.
- **Government-issued ID (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Submit all the requirements to the BPLO and wait for the assessment.
2. Pay the assessed amount and get the official receipt.
3. Return to the BPLO for the Business Closure Certificate.
