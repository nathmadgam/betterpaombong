# Rent Stalls in Public Markets

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Office of the Municipal Market Officer:** 0968-274-6710. Paombong Municipal Market, San Isidro I.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Market-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists market contracts for new tenants, which is how applicants fill vacant stalls at the Paombong Public Market. It also lists contract renewals, stall rentals, cash tickets for ambulant vendors and market clearances. The document gives the requirements and steps.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).

Know how this works in Paombong? [Help write this page](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong).

_Sources: [Municipality of Paombong, Contact Us page](https://paombong.gov.ph/contact-us/), [Citizen's Charter of the Office of the Municipal Market Officer](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Market-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf). The Citizen's Charter was read on 24 November 2026. Spotted an error? [Open an issue](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong/issues)._
