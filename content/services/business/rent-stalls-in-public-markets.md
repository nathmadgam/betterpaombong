# Rent Stalls in Public Markets

How to apply for, renew and pay for a stall at the Paombong Public Market.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Market Officer:** 0968-274-6710. Paombong Municipal Market, San Isidro I.

## Apply for a market stall

The market office processes applicants to fill vacant stalls at the Paombong Public Market.

- **Who can apply:** New applicants aged 18 and above who live in Paombong and are able to do business at the Paombong Public Market.
- **Fee:** Php 6,000.00 for advance and deposit.
- **Processing time:** 5 days and 50 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Barangay residency certificate (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: Your barangay hall.
- **Community Tax Certificate (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: Treasurer's office.
- **Two valid IDs, such as a driver's license, SSS ID or passport (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The agency that issued them.
- **1x1 ID picture.** Where to get it: Any photo center.

### Steps

1. Present all the required documents at the market office.
2. Fill out the application form.
3. Pay the advance and deposit, and accept the official receipt.
4. Review and sign the market contract.
5. Come back after 5 working days, when the contract has been signed by the Municipal Mayor.
6. Sign the logbook to claim the approved market contract.

## Renew a market stall contract

This extends an existing tenant's contract of lease.

- **Who can apply:** Existing tenants of the Paombong Public Market.
- **Fee:** Depends on your existing balance, if any.
- **Processing time:** 5 days and 41 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Barangay residency certificate (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: Your barangay hall.
- **Community Tax Certificate (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: Treasurer's office.
- **Two valid IDs, such as a driver's license, SSS ID or passport (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The agency that issued them.
- **1x1 ID picture.** Where to get it: Any photo center.

### Steps

1. Present all the required documents at the market office.
2. Pay your existing balance, if you have one, and accept the official receipt.
3. Review and sign the market contract.
4. Come back after 5 working days, when the contract has been signed by the Municipal Mayor.
5. Sign the logbook to claim the approved market contract.

## Pay the monthly stall rental

Stall holders pay a monthly rental for their fixed stalls.

- **Who can apply:** Stall holders at the Paombong Public Market.
- **Fee:** The monthly stall fee is set by the Market Code. No amount is printed.
- **Processing time:** 10 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Copy of the official receipt for the previous month.** You provide this.

### Steps

1. Present your latest official receipt.
2. Pay the fee.
3. Accept the official receipt.

## Cash tickets for ambulant vendors

For ambulant vendors who sell in the vicinity of the Paombong Public Market.

- **Who can apply:** Ambulant vendors at the Paombong Public Market.
- **Fee:** Rates are set by the Market Code. No amount is printed.
- **Processing time:** 5 minutes is the printed total. The step times listed add up to 10 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Personal appearance.** No document is needed.

### Steps

1. Approach the assigned market personnel.
2. Pay the fee.
3. Accept the cash tickets.

## Market clearance or certificate

Market tenants ask for this when they renew their Mayor's Permit.

- **Who can apply:** Market tenants in Paombong.
- **Fee:** None for the clearance. An unpaid balance must be settled first, and the amount depends on the balance.
- **Processing time:** 31 minutes.

### What to bring

- **DTI registration (original or photocopy).** Where to get it: Department of Trade and Industry.
- **Barangay clearance (photocopy).** Where to get it: Your barangay hall.
- **Business permit for the previous year (original).** Where to get it: Business Permit and Licensing Office.
- **Updated monthly rental permit.** Where to get it: Paombong Market Office.

### Steps

1. Present all the required documents at the market office.
2. Present your latest official receipt for the monthly payment. If you have an unpaid balance, settle it before the clearance is issued.
3. Receive the market clearance or certificate.
4. Sign the receiving copy.
