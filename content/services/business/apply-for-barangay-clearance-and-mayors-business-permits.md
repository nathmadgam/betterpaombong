# Apply for Barangay Clearance and Mayor's Business Permits

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO):** 0918-938-1967. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/BPLO-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists applications for a new business permit and for renewal, with their requirements and steps. It says the barangay business clearance comes from the barangay hall where the business is located.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).

Know how this works in Paombong? [Help write this page](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong).

_Sources: [Municipality of Paombong, Contact Us page](https://paombong.gov.ph/contact-us/), [Citizen's Charter of the Business Permit and Licensing Office](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/BPLO-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf).
