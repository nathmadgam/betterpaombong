# Apply for a Tricycle Franchise

How tricycle drivers and operators apply for or renew a franchise at the Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO), pay the fee and get a TODA ID.

---

## Where to go

- **Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO):** 0918-938-1967. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Mayor:** (044) 794-0052. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

## Tricycle franchise (new or renewal)

All tricycles in Paombong that are used as public utility vehicles are required to have a franchise. You apply at the BPLO.

- **Who can apply:** Tricycle drivers and operators.
- **Fee:** This document prints no amount, only Municipal Ordinance 2019-02-P-34. The Treasurer's document lists Php 675.00 for a new franchise and Php 575.00 for a renewal.
- **Processing time:** 2 working days and 50 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Tricycle franchise form (1 original).** Where to get it: BPLO.
- **TODA certification (1 original).** Where to get it: The TODA, or tricycle operators and drivers association, that the driver belongs to.
- **OR/CR of the vehicle, meaning its official receipt and certificate of registration (original or photocopy).** Where to get it: Land Transportation Office (LTO).
- **Notarized deed of sale (1 original).** Where to get it: A law firm or notary public.
- **Driver's license (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: Land Transportation Office (LTO).
- **Government-issued IDs of the buyer and the seller (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The agency that issued each ID, such as the PSA, DFA, PRC, COMELEC or BIR.
- **Written authorization allowing the use of the motorcycle, if applicable (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The registered owner.

### Steps

1. Fill out the franchise form and submit it to the BPLO with the other requirements. The BPLO checks them and gives you an Order of Payment.
2. Pay at the Municipal Treasurer's Office and get the official receipt. The document says the cedula is issued at this step too.
3. Return to the BPLO. It checks the documents and the receipt, records them and prints the franchise.
4. Leave the documents and come back on the scheduled date to claim the signed franchise. The document allows 2 working days for this.
5. Receive the franchise together with the franchise plate or sticker, and sign the receiving logbook.
6. Go to the Mayor's Office for the tricycle franchise ID.

The document gives one list for new applications and renewals. It lists the deed of sale and the buyer's and seller's IDs without saying when they apply, so ask the BPLO what you need. The Mayor's Office document has no service called a tricycle franchise ID. It lists a TODA ID, shown below, and does not say whether the two are the same.

## Pay the franchise fee

Tricycle owners and drivers pay the franchise fee at the Treasurer's office. The document describes this service as being for the renewal of a franchise, and it lists a fee for new franchises as well.

- **Who can apply:** All tricycle owners and drivers in the municipality.
- **Fee:** Php 675.00 for a new franchise and Php 575.00 for a renewal, under the Local Revenue Code.
- **Processing time:** 8 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Stub for the assessment of fees (1 copy).** Where to get it: BPLO.

### Steps

1. Present the stub.
2. Pay the fee.
3. Get the official receipt.

## TODA ID

An identification card for members of a Tricycle Operators and Drivers Association (TODA). It serves as proof that the driver or operator is a registered member authorized to operate within the designated area of the municipality.

- **Who can apply:** Registered members of a TODA in the municipality.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 12 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Accomplished application form.**
- **TODA endorsement or certification.** Where to get it: The president of your TODA.
- **Photocopy of a valid ID.** You provide this.
- **Community tax certificate, or cedula.** Where to get it: Municipal Treasurer's Office.

### Steps

1. Submit the application form and all the required documents. The office checks them, then prepares and releases the TODA ID.

The document does not say where to get the application form.
