# Participate in Local Trade Fairs, Business Expos, and Tourism Promotion Activities

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Municipal History, Culture, Arts and Tourism Office:** 0949-994-9587. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

The tourism office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CITIZENCHARTER_TOURISM_final.pdf) lists the management of special events and activities, tourism and cultural materials, and help with Department of Tourism accreditation. It does not mention trade fairs or business expos, so please ask the office.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).

Know how this works in Paombong? [Help write this page](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong).

_Sources: [Municipality of Paombong, Contact Us page](https://paombong.gov.ph/contact-us/), [Citizen's Charter of the Office of the Municipal Mayor, Tourism Operations](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CITIZENCHARTER_TOURISM_final.pdf). The Citizen's Charter was read on 24 November 2026. Spotted an error? [Open an issue](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong/issues)._
