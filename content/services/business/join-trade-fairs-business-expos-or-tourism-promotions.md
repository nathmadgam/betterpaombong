# Participate in Local Trade Fairs, Business Expos, and Tourism Promotion Activities

How to work with the municipal tourism office on a special event, and where to get help with Department of Tourism accreditation.

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal History, Culture, Arts and Tourism Office:** 0949-994-9587. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

The tourism office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CITIZENCHARTER_TOURISM_final.pdf) does not mention trade fairs or business expos, so please ask the office about those.

## Organize a special event with the tourism office

Special events and activities are opportunities for leisure, social or cultural experience outside the usual tourism destinations and attractions in the municipality.

- **Who can apply:** Interested individuals.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 9 days, 2 hours and 5 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Written request addressed to the Municipal Mayor that states what you need.** You write this.
- **Other supporting documents, if necessary.** You provide these.

### Steps

1. Submit the written request.
2. Wait for the approval of the request.
3. Organize the event or activity with the tourism office.

## Help with Department of Tourism accreditation

DOT accreditation is a certification from the Department of Tourism that a tourism enterprise meets the minimum standards for tourism facilities and services. The document says it is mandatory for primary tourism establishments before they start operating.

- **Who can apply:** Interested individuals.
- **Fee:** None at the tourism office. The document does not list the Department of Tourism's own fees.
- **Processing time:** 20 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Written request addressed to the Municipal Mayor that states what you need.** You write this.
- **Other supporting documents, if necessary.** You provide these.

### Steps

1. Submit the written request.
2. Wait for the approval of the request.
