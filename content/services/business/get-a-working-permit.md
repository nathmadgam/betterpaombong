# Get a Working Permit

How employees who work in Paombong get a working permit, also called an occupational permit, at the Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO).

---

## Where to go

- **Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO):** 0918-938-1967. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

## Working permit

A working permit is required for workers and employees who work within Paombong. The document also calls it an occupational permit.

- **Who can apply:** Employees who work within Paombong.
- **Fee:** Php 450.00, under Municipal Ordinance 2019-02-P-34.
- **Processing time:** 30 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Community tax certificate, or cedula (original or photocopy).** Where to get it: Municipal Treasurer's Office.
- **Health ID (1 original).** Where to get it: Rural Health Unit (RHU).
- **Official receipt for the fee (1 original).** Where to get it: Municipal Treasurer's Office, when you pay in step 2.

### Steps

1. Present your cedula and Health ID at the BPLO. The staff then send you to the Treasurer's office to pay.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office and get the official receipt.
3. Return to the BPLO, receive the signed working permit and sign the receiving logbook.

The checklist prints "Original Receipt". This page reads it as the official receipt, which the steps say is issued when you pay.

## Pay the permit fee

The Treasurer's office collects the working permit fee. Its document says the service is for all employed individuals working in establishments within the municipality.

- **Who can apply:** All employed individuals.
- **Fee:** Php 450.00, under the Local Revenue Code, is the fee printed at the payment step, and it matches the BPLO document. The total row of the same table prints Php 675.00 for new and Php 575.00 for renewal, the same amounts as the tricycle franchise fee, so confirm the amount with the office.
- **Processing time:** 8 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Stub (1 copy).** Where to get it: BPLO.

### Steps

1. Present the stub.
2. Pay the fee.
3. Get the official receipt.
