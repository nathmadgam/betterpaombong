# Access Disaster Preparedness Information and Early Warning Systems

How to ask the municipal disaster office (MDRRMO) for hazard information about your area.

---

## Where to go

**In an emergency, call 911**, the national emergency hotline, or the MDRRMO at (044) 761-3199.

- **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO):** (044) 761-3199. MDRRMO Building, Poblacion.

## Ask for hazard information

The MDRRMO provides hazard information (seismic, volcanic, or weather and flood hazards) from available hazard maps, information materials or accredited websites.

- **Who can apply:** Individuals, organizations, barangays and government offices.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 33 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Request letter addressed to the Municipal Mayor, sent through the MDRRMO (2 copies).** You write this.

### Steps

1. Submit the request letter to the MDRRMO.
2. Receive the hazard information.
