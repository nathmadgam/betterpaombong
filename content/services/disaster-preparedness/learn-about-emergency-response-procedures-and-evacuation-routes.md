# Learn About Emergency Response Procedures and Evacuation Routes

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

**In an emergency, call 911**, the national emergency hotline, or the MDRRMO at (044) 761-3199.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO):** (044) 761-3199. MDRRMO Building, Poblacion.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MDRRMO-Arta.pdf) lists responding to emergency calls and requests for hazard information in your locality. It does not publish evacuation routes, so please ask the office or your barangay.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).

Know how this works in Paombong? [Help write this page](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong).
