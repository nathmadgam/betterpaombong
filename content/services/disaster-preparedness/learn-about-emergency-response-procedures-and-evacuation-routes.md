# Learn About Emergency Response Procedures and Evacuation Routes

How to ask the municipal disaster office (MDRRMO) for emergency response, and what its Citizen's Charter says happens next.

---

## Where to go

**In an emergency, call 911**, the national emergency hotline, or the MDRRMO at (044) 761-3199.

- **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO):** (044) 761-3199. MDRRMO Building, Poblacion.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MDRRMO-Arta.pdf) does not publish evacuation routes. Ask the office or your barangay.

## Ask for emergency response

The MDRRM Operation Center assists in search and rescue, firefighting, and medical and trauma incidents.

- **Who can apply:** Anyone: individuals, organizations, barangays and government offices.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 45 minutes is the total listed, from the call to the report on what was done.

### What to bring

- **Details of the incident: what happened, where, the date and the time.** You tell the office.

### Steps

1. Go to the MDRRMO, or text or call its hotline, and ask for emergency response.
2. A standby response team is sent to the place of the incident. It assesses the situation or gives first aid, brings the patient to a health station or the nearest hospital, and endorses the patient to the hospital.
3. The office tells you what was done and the status of the incident.
