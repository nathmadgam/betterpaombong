# Join Disaster Drills and Evacuation Planning

How to ask the municipal disaster office (MDRRMO) for a drill, a training or standby responders for an event.

---

## Where to go

**In an emergency, call 911**, the national emergency hotline, or the MDRRMO at (044) 761-3199.

- **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO):** (044) 761-3199. MDRRMO Building, Poblacion.

## Ask for a drill, a training or standby responders

The MDRRMO conducts trainings and drills, shares disaster information with communities, and provides standby response during mass gatherings, sports activities and similar events that need emergency assistance.

- **Who can apply:** Government offices, barangays, organizations, private groups and individuals.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 1 hour and 8 minutes to receive and answer the request.

### What to bring

- **Request letter addressed to the Municipal Mayor, sent through the MDRRMO (2 copies).** You write this.

### Steps

1. Submit your request letter. The document has the Mayor's Office receive it and the MDRRMO evaluate it.
2. Receive the office's letter with the schedule and anything else it needs.

## Capacity training for an office or group

Training, orientation and knowledge-sharing activities on disaster risk reduction and management.

- **Who can apply:** Government offices, barangays, organizations, private sector partners and individuals.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 1 hour and 3 minutes to receive and answer the request.

### What to bring

- **Request letter addressed to the Municipal Mayor, sent through the MDRRMO (2 copies: 1 original and 1 photocopy).** You write this.
- **List of participants, with sex and age.** You prepare this.

### Steps

1. Send the request letter at least one month before the training.
2. Receive the office's letter with the schedule and the requirements.
