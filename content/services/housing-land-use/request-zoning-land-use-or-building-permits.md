# Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the offices to ask are:

- **Municipal Engineering Office:** (044) 760-4589. Ground floor, Annex Building.
- **Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO):** (044) 760-4596. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

The engineering office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Engineering-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists the building permit and the electrical permit, with their requirements and steps. It says the zoning or locational clearance needed for a building permit comes from the MPDO.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).

Know how this works in Paombong? [Help write this page](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong).

_Sources: [Municipality of Paombong, Contact Us page](https://paombong.gov.ph/contact-us/), [Citizen's Charter of the Municipal Engineering Office](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Engineering-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf).
