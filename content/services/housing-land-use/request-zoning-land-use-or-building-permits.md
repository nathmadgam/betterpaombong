# Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits

How to apply for a building permit and an electrical permit at the Municipal Engineering Office, and where the zoning clearance comes from.

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal Engineering Office:** (044) 760-4589. Ground floor, Annex Building.
- **Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO):** (044) 760-4596. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

The building permit checklist says the zoning or locational clearance comes from the MPDO. The municipality's [Citizen's Charter page](https://paombong.gov.ph/citizens-charter/) has no document for the MPDO yet, so ask that office for its requirements.

## Building permit

A building permit authorizes the construction, alteration or repair of buildings and structures. The office checks that the project follows the National Building Code (Presidential Decree No. 1096) and other regulations.

- **Who can apply:** All applicants.
- **Fee:** Based on the office's fee schedule. No amount is printed.
- **Processing time:** 8 days and 2 hours.

### What to bring

- **Plans, 4 sets, original, signed and sealed: architectural, by a registered architect; structural for two storeys, by a registered civil engineer; structural for three storeys and above, by a registered structural engineer; plumbing or sanitary, by a master plumber or sanitary engineer; electrical, by a professional electrical engineer; mechanical, by a mechanical engineer.**
- **Supporting documents, 4 copies: specifications and cost estimate, signed and sealed by an architect or civil engineer; structural analysis, by a civil engineer for two storeys or a structural engineer for three storeys and above; lot plan.**
- **Photocopies, 4 each: Transfer Certificate of Title, certified true copy; tax declaration or tax receipt for the current year, or an authorization letter; barangay clearance (permit to construct), from the barangay; community tax receipt (CTC) and valid ID; zoning or locational clearance, from the MPDO; F.E.S.E.C., from the fire department.**
- **Forms, 3 sets, original, signed and sealed: building; architectural and structural; sanitary; electrical; mechanical.**
- **PTR and PRC ID of each signatory, 3 photocopies each, with the original signature and seal: civil engineer, architect, professional electrical engineer, sanitary engineer, master plumber, mechanical engineer, professional mechanical engineer.**
- **Other supporting documents: soil boring test, for three storeys and above; Environmental Compliance Certificate, for industrial and commercial projects.**

### Steps

1. Submit the accomplished building permit application form with the complete plans and documents.
2. Pay the required fees at the Treasury or cashier.
3. The office evaluates the plans and supporting documents.
4. The site is inspected, if required.
5. The permit is approved and issued.

## Electrical permit

An electrical permit authorizes the installation, alteration or repair of electrical systems in buildings or structures, under the National Building Code and the Philippine Electrical Code.

- **Who can apply:** Property owners, authorized representatives and contractors.
- **Fee:** Php 350.00.
- **Processing time:** 3 days and 30 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Barangay clearance (original).** Where to get it: The barangay captain of the area.
- **Residence certificate (cedula).** You provide this.
- **Latest tax receipt (1 photocopy).** You provide this.
- **Wiring permit.** You provide this.

### Steps

1. Sign the logbook and tell the office what you need.
2. Submit the requirements.
3. Pay the assessed fee.
4. Present the official receipt.
5. Request an inspection after the installation.

After the final electrical inspection, the office prepares a Certificate of Final Electrical Inspection and sends it to Meralco for the service connection.
