# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup

How to ask the municipal environment office (MENRO) to collect garbage.

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO):** (044) 769-4569. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MENRO-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) does not publish a collection schedule. Ask the office for the schedule in your barangay.

## Ask for garbage to be collected

The MENRO provides regular garbage collection and transport. You can report garbage that needs to be collected.

- **Who can apply:** All applicants. The document lists transactions with citizens, businesses and government offices.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 10 minutes at the office. The document does not say when the collection happens.

### What to bring

- **Letter addressed to the MENRO asking for garbage collection.** You write this.
- **A printed or phone photo of the garbage that shows where it is.** You provide this.
- **For a business: updated business permit (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: Business Permit and Licensing Office.

### Steps

1. Go to the MENRO and sign the visitor logbook.
2. Say what you need.
3. Go with the MENRO staff to locate the garbage.
4. Wait until the garbage has been collected.
