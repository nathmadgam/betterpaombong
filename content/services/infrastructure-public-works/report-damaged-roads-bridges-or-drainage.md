# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Municipal Engineering Office:** (044) 760-4589. Ground floor, Annex Building.

Under the [Local Government Code](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/) (Section 477), the municipal engineer's office is in charge of the maintenance and repair of the municipality's roads, bridges and other public works. The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Engineering-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) covers building and electrical permits only. It does not describe how to report damage, so please call or visit the office.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).
