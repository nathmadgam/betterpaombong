# Use Public Facilities Like Gyms, Barangay Halls, or Multipurpose Centers

How to reserve the municipal covered court or function hall.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Mayor:** (044) 794-0052. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

For a barangay hall or a barangay court, ask the barangay that runs it.

## Reserve the municipal covered court or function hall

Individuals, organizations and community groups may use the municipal covered court and function hall for activities in the interest of public service, community development and civic engagement. Use depends on availability, municipal policies and approval.

- **Who can apply:** Anyone.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 5 minutes to receive the request.

### What to bring

- **Letter of request addressed to the Mayor.** You write this.

### Steps

1. Submit the letter of request to the Office of the Municipal Mayor.
2. The office puts the event in its calendar of activities and has the request signed by the Municipal Mayor.
