# Rent Stalls in Public Markets or Use LGU-Managed Cemeteries

What to pay and bring for a cemetery lot, an exhumation or a transfer, and where to go for a market stall.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Market Officer:** 0968-274-6710. Paombong Municipal Market, San Isidro I.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

For market stalls, see [Rent stalls in public markets](../business/rent-stalls-in-public-markets), which has the requirements, fees and steps.

## Rent a cemetery lot

For those who do not own a memorial lot for a departed relative. The fee is paid at the Office of the Municipal Treasurer.

- **Who can apply:** Anyone in the municipality.
- **Fee:** Php 10,000.00, payable within 5 years, plus a Php 500.00 maintenance fee after 5 years of payment, under the Local Revenue Code.
- **Processing time:** 8 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Stub (1 copy), approved by the cemetery caretaker.** Where to get it: Cemetery caretaker.

### Steps

1. Present the stub approved by the cemetery caretaker.
2. Pay the fee.
3. Accept the official receipt.

## Pay for an exhumation, a bone transfer or the transfer of a cadaver

These fees are paid at the Office of the Municipal Treasurer after the health office issues the permit.

- **Who can apply:** Anyone in the municipality.
- **Fee:** Php 300.00 for a bone transfer, Php 500.00 per vault for an exhumation, and Php 200.00 for the transfer of a cadaver.
- **Processing time:** 8 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Copy of the permit for the exhumation, bone transfer or transfer of cadaver.** Where to get it: Office of the Municipal Health Officer.

### Steps

1. Present a copy of the permit.
2. Pay the fee.
3. Accept the official receipt.
