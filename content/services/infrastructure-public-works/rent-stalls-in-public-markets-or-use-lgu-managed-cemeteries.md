# Rent Stalls in Public Markets or Use LGU-Managed Cemeteries

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the offices to ask are:

- **Office of the Municipal Market Officer:** 0968-274-6710. Paombong Municipal Market, San Isidro I.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

For market stalls, the market office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Market-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists market contracts for new tenants and for renewal, stall rentals and cash tickets for ambulant vendors at the Paombong Public Market.

For the cemetery, the Treasurer's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Treasurer_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists the payment for renting a cemetery lot. It asks for a stub approved by the cemetery caretaker.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).
