# Pay Real Property Tax (Amilyar) and Get a Tax Clearance

How to pay real property tax, often called amilyar, and how to get a real property tax clearance at the Office of the Municipal Treasurer.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Assessor:** (044) 764-3372. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

You pay at the Treasurer's office. If you need a copy of your tax declaration, it comes from the Assessor's office.

## Pay real property tax

Real property tax is charged on all types of real property, including land, buildings, improvements and machinery.

- **Who can apply:** All owners of real property in the municipality.
- **Fee:** The document prints the tax as: Assessed Value x 1% x 2 (Basic & SEF). That is 1% of the property's assessed value for the basic tax, plus another 1% for the Special Education Fund (SEF).
- **Processing time:** 15 minutes per transaction.

### What to bring

- **Your latest official receipt (1 copy).** You provide this.
- **Or, in place of the receipt, your latest tax declaration (1 copy).** Where to get it: Municipal Assessor's Office.

### Steps

1. Present your latest official receipt or your latest tax declaration. The office finds the property in its system and computes the amount.
2. Pay the amount.
3. Get the official receipt.

The Special Education Fund tax is an additional 1% of the assessed value under the [Local Government Code](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/) (Section 235).

## Get a real property tax clearance

A tax clearance is issued to owners of real property. It certifies that their properties have no outstanding dues.

- **Who can apply:** All owners of real property in the municipality.
- **Fee:** Php 200.00 per copy.
- **Processing time:** 8 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Latest and updated tax receipt (1 photocopy).** You or your representative provide this.
- **Special power of attorney (SPA) or authorization letter.** You or your representative provide this.
- **Photocopy of the IDs of the owner, the seller, the buyer and the SPA holder.** You or your representative provide this.
- **Deed of sale and community tax certificate, or cedula (1 photocopy).** You or your representative provide this.

### Steps

1. Present the requirements. The office checks them and prepares the tax clearance.
2. Pay the fee.
3. Receive the tax clearance.

The document lists all of these papers in one row. It does not say which ones you can leave out, for example when there is no sale or when you apply in person, so ask the office what applies to you.
