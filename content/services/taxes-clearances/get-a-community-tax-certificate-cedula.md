# Get a Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)

How to get a community tax certificate, also called a cedula, at the Office of the Municipal Treasurer.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

## Community tax certificate (cedula)

A community tax certificate (CTC), also called a cedula, is a basic document that identifies you and where you live. It can be used for legal transactions.

- **Who can apply:** Everyone aged 18 and above in the municipality.
- **Fee:** For the unemployed: Php 2.00 basic plus Php 25.00 voluntary. For the employed: Php 1.00 for every Php 1,000.00 of earnings. Interest of 2% per month is added starting March.
- **Processing time:** 10 minutes.

### What to bring

- **At least one valid ID.** Where to get it: The government agency that issued the ID.
- **Information form.** Where to get it: Office of the Municipal Treasurer.

### Steps

1. Present a valid ID and fill out the information form for the certificate.
2. Sign the certificate and affix your thumbmark.
3. Pay the fee.
4. Receive your community tax certificate.

The document also says a "juridical being" can get a certificate. Under the [Local Government Code](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/) (Section 158), corporations pay a community tax too.
