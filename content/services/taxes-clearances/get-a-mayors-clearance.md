# Get a Mayor's Clearance

How to get a Mayor's clearance at the Office of the Municipal Mayor, and where the fee is paid.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Mayor:** (044) 794-0052. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

## Mayor's clearance

A Mayor's clearance certifies that a person is a resident of the municipality, has good moral character and is a law-abiding citizen. It is commonly required for employment and for other legal purposes.

- **Who can apply:** Everyone.
- **Fee:** Php 200.00.
- **Processing time:** 11 minutes.

### What to bring

- **"Community Tax Clearance", as the document words it.** Where to get it: Municipal Treasurer's Office.
- **Barangay clearance.** Where to get it: Your barangay hall.
- **Police clearance.** Where to get it: Municipal Police Station.
- **Official receipt for your payment.** Where to get it: Municipal Treasurer's Office.

### Steps

1. Complete all the requirements. The office checks that they are complete and signed.
2. Present your payment receipt at the Mayor's Office. The clearance is then prepared.
3. Sign the clearance. It is then signed by the Municipal Mayor.
4. Receive your Mayor's clearance.

The Treasurer's document has no service called a community tax clearance. It lists the community tax certificate, or [cedula](get-a-community-tax-certificate-cedula), and the [real property tax clearance](pay-real-property-tax-and-get-a-tax-clearance). Ask the Mayor's office which one it needs.

The document prints the Php 200.00 fee beside step 2 and names a cashier there. It does not say where you pay. The receipt it asks for comes from the Municipal Treasurer's Office, and the Treasurer's steps for that payment are below.

## Pay the fee at the Treasurer's office

The Treasurer's office collects the payment for certifications and certified true copies. Its document names the Mayor's clearance as one of them, together with the tax declaration and the certifications of no improvement and of property holdings.

- **Who can apply:** All registered citizens of the municipality.
- **Fee:** Php 200 per certification. The document adds that other fees depend on the assessment on the stub issued by the office concerned.
- **Processing time:** 9 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Assessment stub (1 copy).** Where to get it: Office of the Municipal Assessor or Office of the Municipal Public Employment Service Officer, as the document lists them.

### Steps

1. Present the assessment stub. The office fills out a form for the person making the request.
2. Pay the fee.
3. Get the official receipt.

The document does not say which of the two offices issues the stub for a Mayor's clearance, so ask at the Mayor's office before you pay.
