# Register a Death

How to register a death at the Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar, on time or late, and the burial and transfer fees.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar:** (044) 760-4599. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

A death must be registered in the municipality where it happened, within 30 days. The form is the Certificate of Death (Municipal Form No. 103).

Fees are paid at the Municipal Treasurer's Office, which the document calls the MTO. You then bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office. The Treasurer's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Treasurer_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) says to bring the assessment stub from the civil registrar when you pay.

## Register a death on time

For a death that is registered within 30 days. The office records the Certificate of Death, gives it a registry number and signs it.

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, or an authorized representative with a special power of attorney (SPA) or authorization letter and the valid government-issued IDs of both. The document says they must be 18 years old and above.
- **Fee:** Php 200.00 marked "burial" and Php 200.00 marked "transfer". The total row prints Php 400.00, which is both together. The checklist asks for a transfer permit, a burial permit, or both, and the document does not say whether each amount is charged only when that permit is needed.
- **Processing time:** 46 minutes, the printed total.

### What to bring

- **Accomplished Certificate of Death.** Where to get it: The hospital, the rural health unit (RHU) or the funeral service.
- **Transfer permit, burial permit, or both.**

### Steps

1. Submit the Certificate of Death with the requirements. If they are complete, the office records the document, assigns a registry number and signs it.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office for recording.
3. Receive the registered document.

The document does not clearly say where the transfer permit and the burial permit come from, so ask the civil registry office. If the requirements are not complete, the office gives you a checklist and asks you to submit the rest.

## Register a death late

For a death that was not registered within 30 days. The document calls this delayed registration.

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, or an authorized representative with a special power of attorney (SPA) or authorization letter and the valid government-issued IDs of both. The document says they must be 18 years old and above.
- **Fee:** The same table lists Php 200.00 marked "burial" and Php 200.00 marked "transfer". It prints no separate fee for a delayed registration.
- **Processing time:** 46 minutes is the printed total at the office. Delayed registrations are released after a 10-day posting period.

### What to bring

- **PSA No Record of Death.** Where to get it: PSA.
- **Accomplished Certificate of Death, signed by the doctor, the attending physician, the Municipal Health Officer or a relative.** Where to get it: The hospital, the rural health unit (RHU) or the funeral service.
- **Certificate of Burial.** Where to get it: The cemetery or memorial park where the deceased was buried.
- **Certification from the church.** Where to get it: The church where the memorial service was held.
- **Affidavit for Delayed Registration of Death, by relatives, with valid IDs.** Where to get it: A notary public.
- **Affidavit of Two Disinterested Persons, with valid IDs.** Where to get it: A notary public.
- **Other documents, if necessary.**
- **Picture of the lapida, the grave marker, if necessary.** Where to get it: A relative of the deceased.
- **If the person filing is not an immediate family member: an SPA or authorization letter from an immediate family member, with the government-issued valid IDs of both.** Where to get it: The immediate family member and a notary public.

### Steps

1. Submit the accomplished Certificate of Death with the requirements. If they are complete, the office records the document, assigns a registry number and signs it.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office for recording.
3. Receive the registered document after the 10-day posting period.

If the requirements are not complete, the office gives you a checklist and asks you to submit the rest.
