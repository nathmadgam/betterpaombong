# Correct or Complete a Civil Registry Record

How to correct a clerical error, a first name, the day or month of birth, or the sex in a civil registry record without going to court, and how to add an entry that was left out.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar:** (044) 760-4599. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

Fees are paid at the Municipal Treasurer's Office, which the document calls the MTO. You then bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office. The Treasurer's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Treasurer_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) says to bring the assessment stub from the civil registrar when you pay.

Where the document prints PSA, CCR, LCR as the source of a certificate, this page says the PSA or a civil registrar. The PSA is the Philippine Statistics Authority.

## Correct an entry (RA 9048 and RA 10172)

Republic Act 9048 lets the civil registrar correct a clerical or typographical error, or change a first name, without a court order. Republic Act 10172 added the correction of the day and month of birth, and of sex. The office accepts your petition and sends it to the PSA Central Office for a decision. The requirements depend on your case and on which supporting documents are available.

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, or an authorized representative with an authorization letter or SPA and the valid government-issued IDs of both, who must be 18 years old and above. For a change of sex, the document owner must appear in person.
- **Fee:** Php 1,000 for the correction of a clerical error and Php 3,000 for a change of first name or of sex. Php 500 is listed for migrant petitioners. The checklist adds "+ misc." to the first two without giving an amount. The table names no fee for correcting the day or month of birth. The Treasurer's document lists Php 1,000.00 under RA 9048 and Php 3,000.00 under RA 10172, so confirm the amount with the office.
- **Processing time:** About 4 to 6 months for the whole process, as the office tells petitioners. The printed total for the steps at the office is 101 minutes.

### What to bring

- **PSA record of the birth, marriage or death.** Where to get it: PSA.
- **Baptismal certificate.** Where to get it: The church where the child was baptized.
- **School record (Form 137), or a certification if the record is destroyed or unavailable.** Where to get it: The school.
- **Comelec record, SSS or GSIS record.** Where to get it: Comelec, SSS, GSIS.
- **PhilHealth MDR, land title.** Where to get it: PhilHealth, the Assessor.
- **Passport, driver's license, PRC ID, National ID.** Where to get it: DFA, LTO, PRC, PSA.
- **Medical record, business record.** Where to get it: A hospital or clinic, the BPLO.
- **Birth certificate of the mother, father, siblings, children, grandparents.** Where to get it: The PSA or a civil registrar.
- **Marriage certificate of the parents, of the petitioner.** Where to get it: The PSA or a civil registrar.
- **NBI clearance, police clearance, barangay clearance, cedula.** Where to get it: NBI, the police, your barangay and the municipal government.
- **Valid government-issued ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.
- **Certificate of employment, or an affidavit of non-employment or of self-employment.** Where to get it: Your employer, or a notary public.
- **Affidavit of newspaper publication, for 2 consecutive weeks of publication.** Where to get it: The publisher of a newspaper with national circulation.
- **Other documents, if necessary.**
- **1 long folder.**
- **If a representative files: an SPA or authorization letter from the document owner, with the government-issued valid IDs of both. This is not allowed for a change of sex.** Where to get it: The document owner and a notary public.

### Steps

1. Submit all the requirements. If they are complete, the office accepts the petition and tells you that the process takes about 4 to 6 months.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and bring the official receipt back for recording. The staff help you sign the petition form, assign a petition number, and send the petition and the supporting documents to the PSA Central Office Legal Division for decision.
3. Wait to be notified of the PSA's decision, then receive the approved petition. The office advises you to request the document with the annotation at the PSA Regional Office.

The document separates many of these papers with a slash and does not say which ones go with which kind of correction, so ask the office what your petition needs. If the requirements are not complete, the office gives you a checklist and asks you to submit the rest.

## Supplemental report for a missing entry

A supplemental report supplies an entry or information that was left out by mistake when a birth, marriage or death was registered.

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, a parent if the owner is a minor, or an authorized representative with an SPA or authorization letter and the government-issued IDs of both.
- **Fee:** Php 200.00. You also cover the courier fee.
- **Processing time:** 59 minutes.

### What to bring

- **The PSA copy of the birth, marriage or death certificate to be supplemented (original and 4 photocopies).** Where to get it: PSA.
- **Baptismal certificate (original and 4 photocopies).** Where to get it: The church where you were baptized.
- **Marriage certificate of the owner or of the parents (original and 4 photocopies).** Where to get it: The PSA or a civil registrar.
- **Government-issued valid IDs (4 photocopies with countersignature).** Where to get it: The agency that issued each ID.
- **Notarized Affidavit of Supplemental Report (2 originals and 4 photocopies).** Where to get it: A notary public.
- **Other requirements, if necessary.**
- **If a representative applies: an SPA or authorization letter, with the government-issued valid IDs of both (1 photocopy with countersignature).** Where to get it: The document owner and a notary public.

### Steps

1. Submit all the requirements. If they are complete, the office prepares and signs the documents for the PSA in San Fernando, Pampanga. If more than 3 entries were omitted, they are forwarded to the PSA Central Office in Manila by courier.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office for recording. You also cover the courier fee.
3. Receive your copy. You present it when you request a copy with annotations at the PSA.
