# Have a Record Endorsed to the PSA

What to do when the PSA has no copy of a birth, marriage or death record that is registered at the Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar:** (044) 760-4599. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

Fees are paid at the Municipal Treasurer's Office, which the document calls the MTO. You then bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office. The Treasurer's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Treasurer_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) says to bring the assessment stub from the civil registrar when you pay.

The PSA is the Philippine Statistics Authority. The document calls its database the Civil Registry System (CRS).

## Endorsement of a record to the PSA

For a record that cannot be found in the CRS database and archives, whether it was registered long ago or recently. The civil registry office prepares the documents and sends them to the PSA in Plaridel, Bulacan by courier.

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, or an authorized representative with a special power of attorney (SPA) or authorization letter and the valid government-issued IDs of both. The document says they must be 18 years old and above.
- **Fee:** Php 200.00. You also cover the courier fee.
- **Processing time:** 61 minutes.

### What to bring

- **PSA No Record (original and 4 photocopies).** Where to get it: PSA.
- **The owner's government-issued valid ID (4 photocopies with countersignature).** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.
- **Other requirements, if necessary.**
- **If a representative applies: an SPA or authorization letter from the document owner, with the government-issued valid IDs of both (1 photocopy with countersignature).**

### Steps

1. Submit all the requirements. If they are complete, the office prepares and signs the documents for submission to the PSA in Plaridel, Bulacan by courier.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office for recording. You also cover the courier fee.
3. Receive your copy. You present it when you request a copy with annotations at the PSA.

## Advance copy for a newly registered record

For a record that is registered at the civil registry office but has not yet been received and encoded in the CRS database. You write a letter that explains your urgent need for the document.

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, or an authorized representative with a special power of attorney (SPA) or authorization letter and the valid government-issued IDs of both. The document says they must be 18 years old and above.
- **Fee:** Php 200.00. You also cover the courier fee.
- **Processing time:** 61 minutes is the printed total. The step times listed add up to 71 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Letter of request from the owner, explaining the urgent need for the document (original and 3 photocopies).** Where to get it: The document owner.
- **The registered birth, marriage or death certificate (original and 3 photocopies).** Where to get it: A civil registrar.
- **The owner's government-issued valid ID (4 photocopies with countersignature).** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.
- **Other requirements, if necessary.**
- **If a representative applies: an SPA or authorization letter, with the government-issued valid IDs of both (1 photocopy with countersignature).**

### Steps

1. Submit all the requirements. If they are complete, the office prepares and signs the documents for submission to the PSA in Plaridel, Bulacan by courier.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office for recording. You also cover the courier fee.
3. Receive your copy. You present it when you request a copy with annotations at the PSA.
