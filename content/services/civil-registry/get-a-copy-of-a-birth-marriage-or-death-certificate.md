# Get a Copy of a Birth, Marriage or Death Certificate

How to get a copy of a birth, marriage or death certificate from the Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar, and how to have a copy certified.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar:** (044) 760-4599. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

Fees are paid at the Municipal Treasurer's Office, which the document calls the MTO. You then bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office. The Treasurer's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Treasurer_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) says to bring the assessment stub from the civil registrar when you pay.

## Copy from the civil registry's computer system

The office issues birth, marriage and death certificates from its computer database, the Civil Registry Information System (CRIS). The copy is on Municipal Form 1A for a birth, 3A for a marriage and 2A for a death.

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, or an authorized representative with a special power of attorney (SPA) or authorization letter and the valid government-issued IDs of both. The document says they must be 18 years old and above.
- **Fee:** Php 200.00, based on the Local Revenue Code of 2019.
- **Processing time:** 41 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Complete information about the document you are requesting.** You write this down at the civil registry office.
- **Government-issued valid ID of the document owner, and of the representative if there is one.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.
- **If a representative applies: an SPA or authorization letter from the document owner, with the government-issued valid IDs of both.** Where to get it: The document owner and a notary public.

### Steps

1. Write down the document you need, with complete information. The staff ask for your valid ID and check the record. If it is available, they print and sign the copy.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and present the official receipt to the civil registry staff for recording.
3. Receive the document.

If the record is not available, the office advises you to request a copy from the PSA. If the PSA has none either, it advises you to file a late registration and gives you the list of requirements.

## Certified true copy

An officially verified photocopy of a birth, marriage or death certificate. An authorized official authenticates it to confirm that it is an exact and legal copy of the original. The document's title prints this as "certified thru copy".

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, or an authorized representative with a special power of attorney (SPA) or authorization letter and the valid government-issued IDs of both. The document says they must be 18 years old and above.
- **Fee:** Php 200.00.
- **Processing time:** 45 minutes.

### What to bring

- **The original civil registry copy of the certificate to be certified: birth (Form 102), marriage (Form 97) or death (Form 103).** Where to get it: Your own copy, or the civil registry office.
- **The document owner's government-issued valid ID (1 photocopy with countersignature).** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.
- **If a representative applies: an SPA or authorization letter, with the government-issued valid IDs of both (1 photocopy with countersignature).** Where to get it: The document owner and a notary public.

### Steps

1. Submit the document. The staff check that it is authentic, then prepare and sign the documents to be certified.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office for recording.
3. Receive the certified documents.

The document says the number of documents to be certified depends on what you need. It does not say whether the fee is charged for each one.
