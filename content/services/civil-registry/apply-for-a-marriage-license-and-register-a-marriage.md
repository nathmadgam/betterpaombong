# Apply for a Marriage License and Register a Marriage

How to apply for a marriage license at the Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar, where the pre-marriage counseling session fits in, and how a marriage is registered.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Civil Registrar:** (044) 760-4599. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Office of the Municipal Treasurer:** 0925-305-3076. Ground floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Municipal Population and Development Office:** 0922-633-9593. 2nd floor, Rural Health Unit I, Poblacion.

Fees are paid at the Municipal Treasurer's Office, which the document calls the MTO. You then bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office. The Treasurer's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Treasurer_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) says to bring the assessment stub from the civil registrar when you pay.

Where the document prints PSA, CCR, LCR as the source of a certificate, this page says the PSA or a civil registrar. The PSA is the Philippine Statistics Authority.

## Marriage license

You apply with the Application for Marriage License (Municipal Form No. 90). Both applicants must appear in person at the civil registrar of the place where either of them lives. The license is released after a ten-day posting period.

- **Who can apply:** Couples who are 18 years old and above, where at least one of them lives in Paombong.
- **Fee:** The payment step lists Php 300.00 for the application, Php 100.00 for family planning and Php 100.00 for pre-marriage counseling. The total row prints Php 600.00, which is more than those three add up to.
- **Processing time:** 62 minutes at the office, the printed total. The license is released after the ten-day posting period.

### What to bring

- **Accomplished Application for Marriage License form.** Where to get it: The civil registry office.
- **Birth certificate (original and 1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The PSA or a civil registrar.
- **PSA CENOMAR, the certificate of no marriage record (original and 1 photocopy).** Where to get it: PSA.
- **Barangay certificate of residency or clearance (original).** Where to get it: The barangay where you live.
- **Valid IDs with address, and cedula (original and photocopies).** Where to get it: The agency that issued each ID, and the Municipal Treasurer's Office for the cedula.
- **2x2 colored ID pictures (2 pieces).** Where to get it: A photo shop.
- **Other documents, if necessary.**
- **If you are 18 to 20 years old: your father appears to sign the Parental Consent Form, which is available at the civil registry office, and brings his valid ID.**
- **If you are 21 to 24 years old: your parents appear to sign the Parental Advice Form, which is available at the civil registry office, and bring their valid IDs.**
- **Affidavit of Guardianship, if necessary.** Where to get it: A notary public.
- **Foreigners, in addition: passport with the date of arrival (original and 2 photocopies).** Where to get it: DFA, as the document prints it.
- **Foreigners, in addition: certificate of legal capacity from the diplomatic or consular official in the Philippines (original and 2 photocopies).** Where to get it: Your embassy or consulate in the Philippines.
- **Foreigners, if divorced or annulled: court order and certificate of finality or judicial decree (2 certified photocopies).** Where to get it: The court.
- **Foreigners, if divorced or annulled: PSA marriage certificate with the annotation of the annulment or divorce (original and 2 photocopies).** Where to get it: PSA.
- **Widow or widower, in addition: PSA death certificate of the spouse (original and 2 photocopies).** Where to get it: The PSA or a civil registrar.
- **Widow or widower, in addition: PSA certificate of marriage to the previous spouse (original and 2 photocopies).** Where to get it: The PSA or a civil registrar.

### Steps

1. Appear together at the civil registry office and submit the requirements with the accomplished application. The office checks them and interviews you, and your parents if needed, then prepares the application for you to sign.
2. Pay the fees at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and present the official receipt to the civil registry staff. They send you to the Population Office for the schedule of the family planning and pre-marriage counseling sessions.
3. Receive the marriage license, with the certificate of family planning and pre-marriage counseling, after the ten-day posting period.

The document lists the papers for a divorced or annulled applicant under the additional requirements for foreigners. If that is your case and you are Filipino, ask the office what it needs.

The Treasurer's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Treasurer_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists Php 300.00 for the application for marriage and for legitimation, Php 100.00 for parental advice or consent, pre-marriage counseling and family planning, and Php 600.00 for solemnization. The two documents do not fully agree, so ask the civil registry office for the total before you pay.

If the requirements are not complete, the office gives you a checklist and asks you to submit the rest.

## Pre-marriage orientation and counseling

Couples applying for a marriage license must first attend a Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling session. The Municipal Population and Development Office gives the session and issues the certificate of compliance.

- **Who can apply:** People applying for a marriage license.
- **Fee:** None at this office. The document says the fees are payable when you apply for the marriage license at the civil registrar's office.
- **Processing time:** 2 hours and 40 minutes, including the 2-hour session.

### What to bring

- **Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling Information Form.** Where to get it: The civil registry office.
- **Marriage Expectation Inventory Form.** Where to get it: Municipal Population and Development Office.

### Steps

1. Submit the accomplished Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling form. The office reviews it and interviews you for the schedule of the session.
2. Attend the Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling session.
3. Receive the Certificate of Compliance for Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling.

## Register a marriage on time

A marriage must be registered in the municipality where it took place, within 15 days after the solemnization. The form is the Certificate of Marriage (Municipal Form No. 97).

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, or an authorized representative with a special power of attorney (SPA) or authorization letter and the valid government-issued IDs of both. The document says they must be 18 years old and above.
- **Fee:** The document prints no amount for registering a marriage, although its steps include paying a fee. The table's only amounts are Php 200.00 marked "burial" and Php 200.00 marked "transfer", and its total row prints Php 400.00. The page on registering a death covers those. Ask the office what you will be charged.
- **Processing time:** 46 minutes, the printed total.

### What to bring

- **Accomplished Certificate of Marriage.** Where to get it: The solemnizing officer.
- **Photocopies of the application for marriage license, the family planning and pre-marriage counseling certificate, and other supporting documents, attached.** Where to get it: The civil registrar where the license was obtained.
- **Certificate of the solemnizing officer (photocopy).** Where to get it: The solemnizing officer.

### Steps

1. Submit the Certificate of Marriage with the requirements. If they are complete, the office records the document, assigns a registry number and signs it.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office for recording.
3. Receive the registered document.

## Register a marriage late

For a marriage that was not registered within 15 days. The document calls this delayed registration.

- **Who can apply:** The document owner, or an authorized representative with a special power of attorney (SPA) or authorization letter and the valid government-issued IDs of both. The document says they must be 18 years old and above.
- **Fee:** The document prints no amount for a delayed registration of marriage, although its steps include paying a fee. The table's only amounts are Php 200.00 marked "burial" and Php 200.00 marked "transfer", and its total row prints Php 400.00. The page on registering a death covers those. Ask the office what you will be charged.
- **Processing time:** 46 minutes is the printed total at the office. Delayed registrations are released after a 10-day posting period.

### What to bring

- **PSA No Record of Marriage.** Where to get it: PSA.
- **PSA CENOMAR of the husband and of the wife.** Where to get it: PSA.
- **The marriage license that was issued, and the record of marriage from the solemnizing officer.** Where to get it: The civil registrar where the license was obtained, and the solemnizing officer for the record of marriage.
- **Picture of the wedding ceremony, if possible.** You provide this.
- **Certificates of birth of the children, with the parents' date of marriage.** Where to get it: The PSA or a civil registrar.
- **Accomplished Certificate of Marriage.** Where to get it: The solemnizing officer.
- **Affidavit of the solemnizing officer and certificate of the solemnizing officer.** Where to get it: A notary public.
- **Affidavit for Delayed Registration of Marriage, by the husband and wife.** Where to get it: A notary public.
- **Affidavit of Two Disinterested Persons, with valid IDs.** Where to get it: A notary public.
- **Letter of request from the solemnizing officer to the Municipal Civil Registrar for the delayed registration.** Where to get it: The solemnizing officer.
- **Valid IDs of the husband and wife.** Where to get it: The agency that issued each ID.
- **Other documents, if necessary.**
- **If a representative files: an SPA or authorization letter from the document owner, with the government-issued valid IDs of both.** Where to get it: The document owner and a notary public.

### Steps

1. Submit the accomplished Certificate of Marriage with the requirements. If they are complete, the office records the document, assigns a registry number and signs it.
2. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) and bring the official receipt back to the civil registry office for recording.
3. Receive the registered document after the 10-day posting period.

If the requirements are not complete, the office gives you a checklist and asks you to submit the rest.
