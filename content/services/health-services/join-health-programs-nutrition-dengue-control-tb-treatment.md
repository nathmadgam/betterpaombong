# Join Health Programs (Nutrition, Dengue Control, TB Treatment)

Tuberculosis care, nutrition services for young children and pregnant women, and medical equipment for indigent patients.

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal Health Office:** (044) 760-4041. Rural Health Unit I, Poblacion.
- **Municipal Nutrition Office:** Not listed. 2nd floor, Rural Health Unit I, Poblacion.
- **Office of the Municipal Mayor:** (044) 794-0052. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Rural Health Unit II, San Isidro II:** (044) 764-6101.

The nutrition services are from the Municipal Nutrition Office, on the 2nd floor of Rural Health Unit I. The municipality's Contact Us page lists no phone number for it.

The health office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MHO-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) does not list a dengue control program. It lists a dengue NS1 rapid test among its laboratory tests: see [free check-ups, laboratory tests and vaccines](get-free-check-ups-basic-medicines-and-vaccines).

## Tuberculosis services

Free tuberculosis screening, diagnosis, treatment and follow-up. The document lists consultation, laboratory testing, directly observed treatment, monitoring and health education.

- **Who can apply:** Paombong residents who have signs and symptoms of tuberculosis (such as a cough lasting two weeks or more, fever, weight loss or night sweats), who live with or are close contacts of a confirmed patient, who are referred by a health facility, who need a tuberculosis clearance, or who were treated before and need follow-up.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 1 hour and 59 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Previous medical records or laboratory results, if available.** Where to get it: Health office or your previous health facility.
- **Sputum sample, for clients with symptoms.** You provide this during the visit.
- **Tuberculosis Treatment Card.** Where to get it: Municipal Health Office.
- **NTP Form 7 or referral slip, if another health facility referred you.** Where to get it: The referring hospital or clinic.
- **Valid ID, such as a National ID, driver's license, UMID, passport, voter's ID, senior citizen ID, PWD ID or postal ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Sign the patient's consent for medical treatment.
2. Wait in the waiting area for your queue number.
3. See the doctor at the Out-Patient Department clinic room.
4. Go to the nurse's room.
5. Name your close contacts for contact tracing.

For close contacts, the document lists a tuberculin skin test for children below 5 years old and chest X-ray vouchers for people 15 years old and above.

## Weighing and nutrition assessment (Operation Timbang Plus)

Registration and nutrition assessment of unregistered children aged 0 to 59 months. The child's weight and height or length are measured to find the child's nutritional status.

- **Who can apply:** Paombong families with unregistered children aged 0 to 59 months.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 20 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Baby Book or Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD) booklet.** Where to get it: Barangay health center, from the rural health midwife.

### Steps

1. Present the child's Baby Book or ECCD booklet.
2. Wait for the results.

## Nutrition counseling

Health and nutrition education for caregivers of children aged 0 to 59 months, including proper feeding, growth monitoring and the prevention of malnutrition.

- **Who can apply:** Parents and caregivers of children aged 0 to 59 months in Paombong.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 20 to 35 minutes.

### What to bring

- **The child's Baby Book or ECCD booklet, if available.** Where to get it: Barangay health center, from the rural health midwife.
- **The parent or caregiver must be present.** No document is needed.

### Steps

1. Appear in person and ask for nutrition counseling.
2. Take part in the counseling session.

## Vitamin and mineral supplements

Essential micronutrient supplements, including vitamins and minerals, for young children and pregnant women.

- **Who can apply:** Children aged 0 to 59 months and pregnant women who live in the municipality.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 15 to 20 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Maternal health record, for pregnant women, if available.** Where to get it: Barangay health center.
- **Baby Book or ECCD booklet, for children.** Where to get it: Barangay health center, from the rural health midwife.

### Steps

1. Appear in person and ask for micronutrient supplementation.
2. Present the records you have.
3. Receive the supplements and instructions.

## Medical equipment for indigent patients

Blood pressure apparatus, nebulizers, glucometers and wheelchairs for qualified residents, to support health monitoring and treatment at home. This is from the Office of the Municipal Mayor.

- **Who can apply:** Indigent residents; patients with hypertension, asthma, diabetes or related conditions; senior citizens and PWDs; and individuals endorsed by barangay officials.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 4 days and 25 minutes is the printed total. The step times listed add up to 4 days and 10 to 15 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Barangay certificate of indigency.** Where to get it: Your barangay hall.
- **Valid ID.** Where to get it: Any government-issued ID.
- **Medical certificate.** Where to get it: A government or private physician.
- **Request letter.** You write this.

### Steps

1. Complete all the requirements and submit them to the Office of the Municipal Mayor.
2. Wait for a message from the Office of the Municipal Mayor.
3. Claim the equipment. The document says equipment is claimed every Monday.
