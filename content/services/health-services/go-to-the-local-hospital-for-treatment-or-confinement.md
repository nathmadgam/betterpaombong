# Go to the Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement

Where to go in an emergency or after an animal bite. The Municipal Health Office gives first aid and refers patients to a higher facility.

---

## Where to go

**In an emergency, call 911**, the national emergency hotline, or the MDRRMO at (044) 761-3199.

- **Municipal Health Office:** (044) 760-4041. Rural Health Unit I, Poblacion.
- **Rural Health Unit II, San Isidro II:** (044) 764-6101.

The Department of Health's registry lists no government hospital in Paombong. For the private hospital and the other facilities in town, see [Find a health center, clinic or hospital in Paombong](find-a-health-center-clinic-or-hospital).

## Emergency services at the health office

Treatment and first aid by the health professionals of the Municipal Health Office. The office refers the patient to a higher facility if needed.

- **Who can apply:** Anyone.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 27 minutes.

### Steps

1. Sign the patient's record for medical treatment. The staff record your information and vital signs and do a medical assessment.
2. Wait for the transfer, if the office refers you to a higher facility.

The document lists no requirement for emergency services.

## Animal bite treatment

Treatment for patients bitten or scratched by an animal, including the anti-rabies vaccine.

- **Who can apply:** Paombong residents who need treatment for an animal bite or scratch.
- **Fee:** None listed for the consultation and the anti-rabies vaccine. If you have not had an anti-tetanus shot, the document says to buy the anti-tetanus vaccine at a pharmacy, at Php 100.00.
- **Processing time:** 2 hours and 20 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Photocopy of a valid ID, such as a National ID, driver's license, UMID, passport, voter's ID, senior citizen ID, PWD ID or postal ID (1 copy).** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.
- **Request letter to the Mayor for the anti-rabies vaccine (1 copy).** You write this.

### Steps

1. Sign the patient's consent for medical treatment.
2. Wait in the waiting area for your queue number.
3. See the doctor at the Out-Patient Department clinic room for the bite assessment. You are asked if you have had an anti-tetanus shot. If you have, you get a booster. If not, the document says to buy the anti-tetanus vaccine at a pharmacy.
4. Submit the request letter at the Mayor's Office and receive an Anti-Rabies Card.
5. Return to the Rural Health Unit and present the Anti-Rabies Card.
6. Receive the anti-rabies vaccine.
7. Stay in the observation area until the observation period is over.
