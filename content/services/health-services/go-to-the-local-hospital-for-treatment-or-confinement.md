# Go to the Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, please ask the **Municipal Health Office** about requirements, schedules and fees. It is at Rural Health Unit I, Poblacion.

- **Municipal Health Office:** (044) 760-4041
- **Rural Health Unit II, San Isidro II:** (044) 764-6101

For every health center, barangay health station and hospital in town, see [Find a health center, clinic or hospital in Paombong](find-a-health-center-clinic-or-hospital).
