# Access Maternal Care & Child Immunization

Prenatal care, the birthing station, vaccines, family planning and ultrasound at the Municipal Health Office, plus the maternal kit from the Mayor's office.

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal Health Office:** (044) 760-4041. Rural Health Unit I, Poblacion.
- **Office of the Municipal Mayor:** (044) 794-0052. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Rural Health Unit II, San Isidro II:** (044) 764-6101.

## Prenatal and postnatal care (maternal services)

Prenatal consultation, routine maternal assessment, laboratory requests, immunization for pregnant women, nutrition counseling, birth planning and postnatal follow-up.

- **Who can apply:** Pregnant women, mothers within six weeks after delivery, women planning to become pregnant, and maternal clients referred by other health facilities.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 1 hour and 41 minutes is the printed total. The step times listed add up to 1 hour and 31 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Maternal Health Booklet.** Where to get it: Municipal Health Office.
- **Ultrasound result or laboratory results.** Where to get it: Health office, or a hospital or diagnostic clinic.
- **Valid ID, such as a National ID, driver's license, UMID, passport, voter's ID, senior citizen ID, PWD ID or postal ID.** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.

### Steps

1. Sign the patient's information in the Individual Treatment Record.
2. Wait in the waiting area for your queue number.
3. Go to the maternal consultation area.
4. Receive your updated maternal health record and the schedule of your next appointment.

## Paombong Birthing Station: prenatal consultation

The birthing station provides prenatal consultation, skilled birth attendance for normal deliveries, newborn care, care after birth, PhilHealth assistance and the Certificate of Live Birth.

- **Who can apply:** Pregnant women in Paombong.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 1 hour and 29 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Maternal health record, if you are an existing patient.** Where to get it: Barangay midwife.
- **PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record, if you are a member.** Where to get it: PhilHealth office.
- **Laboratory results, if applicable.** Where to get it: You, your OB-GYN or the laboratory.

### Steps

1. Register at the Birthing Station.
2. Undergo the assessment.
3. Receive the perinatal care services.
4. Be discharged, or referred if the pregnancy is high-risk.

## Paombong Birthing Station: giving birth

For a normal spontaneous delivery at the birthing station, with newborn care and monitoring after birth.

- **Who can apply:** Pregnant women in Paombong who are in labor.
- **Fee:** None. For the PhilHealth step the document says: covered by PhilHealth, subject to LGU policy.
- **Processing time:** 13 hours and 35 minutes is the printed total. The step times listed add up to about 9 hours and 47 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Maternal health record, if you are an existing patient.** Where to get it: Barangay midwife.
- **PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record, if you are a member.** Where to get it: PhilHealth office.
- **Laboratory results, if applicable.** Where to get it: You, your OB-GYN or the laboratory.

### Steps

1. Undergo triage and admission.
2. Give birth.
3. Recover and be monitored after the birth.
4. Process PhilHealth.
5. Receive 3 copies of the Certificate of Live Birth and submit all 3 to the Local Civil Registrar. The document says to register the newborn there within 30 days to avoid a penalty.
6. Be discharged.

## Vaccines (immunization services)

Free routine and campaign immunization to prevent vaccine-preventable diseases.

- **Who can apply:** Infants, children 0 to 4 years old, school-aged children, adolescents, pregnant women and senior citizens.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 29 minutes is the printed total. The step times listed add up to 34 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Immunization card, prenatal card or National Immunization Program card.** Where to get it: Municipal Health Office.

### Steps

1. Submit the immunization or prenatal card.
2. Sign the patient's consent for the immunization session.
3. Wait in the waiting area for your queue number to be called.
4. Receive the vaccine.

## Family planning

Counseling and access to contraceptive methods, including pills, condoms, injectables and long-acting options.

- **Who can apply:** Men and women of reproductive age in Paombong.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 1 hour and 9 minutes for pills, condoms or an injectable. 1 hour and 36 minutes for an IUD or implant.

### What to bring

- **PhilHealth ID, if available.** Where to get it: PhilHealth office.

### Steps

1. Sign the patient's consent for the medical record.
2. Wait for your name to be called. It is first come, first served.
3. Go to the maternal consultation area.
4. Give your consent for the method you choose.
5. Receive the family planning method.
6. Receive follow-up instructions and the schedule of your next appointment.

## Ultrasound

Diagnostic ultrasound, including for pregnancy and abdominal concerns. It needs a doctor's request, and your request form must be signed at the Mayor's Office.

- **Who can apply:** Paombong residents with a physician's request for an ultrasound.
- **Fee:** None for the ultrasound. The document lists Php 10.00 for photocopies, at Php 5.00 per document.
- **Processing time:** 2 days, 2 hours and 13 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Photocopy of a valid ID, such as a National ID, driver's license, UMID, passport, voter's ID, senior citizen ID, PWD ID or postal ID (1 copy).** Where to get it: The agency that issued the ID.
- **Request letter to the Mayor for the ultrasound (1 copy).** You write this.
- **Ultrasound request form, if available (1 original and 1 copy).** Where to get it: Rural health physician.

### Steps

1. Give your information for the Individual Treatment Record.
2. Wait in the waiting area for your queue number.
3. See the doctor at the Out-Patient Department clinic room.
4. Write a request letter to the Mayor, and photocopy the ultrasound request form and a valid ID.
5. Submit the letter and the photocopies to the Mayor's Office, and wait for your request form to be signed.
6. Return to the Super Health Center on the scheduled date and wait for your turn. It is first come, first served.
7. Have the ultrasound examination.
8. Wait for the interpretation and the release of the results.

## Buntis Kit (maternal kit) from the Mayor's office

A kit for pregnant women to support basic maternal and newborn needs. The document says it usually contains essential maternity and newborn care items.

- **Who can apply:** Pregnant women.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 17 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Maternal record or prenatal card.** Where to get it: Health office or barangay health center.
- **Photocopy of a valid ID.** You provide this.

### Steps

1. Submit the required documents to the Office of the Municipal Mayor.
2. Sign the acknowledgment receipt.
