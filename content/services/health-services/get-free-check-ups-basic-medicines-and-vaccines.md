# Get Free Check-ups, Basic Medicines, and Vaccines

Free consultations, vaccines, laboratory tests and dental care at the Municipal Health Office, with what to bring and how long each takes.

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal Health Office:** (044) 760-4041. Rural Health Unit I, Poblacion.
- **Rural Health Unit II, San Isidro II:** (044) 764-6101.

The health office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MHO-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists no fee for these services. It does not say whether medicines are given free, so ask the health office.

## Consultation (out-patient service)

General consultation, assessment, diagnosis, treatment and referral for patients at the Municipal Health Office. The doctor may issue a prescription, laboratory requests, a referral form or a medical certificate.

- **Who can apply:** Anyone.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 1 hour and 1 minute.

### Steps

1. Sign the patient daily logbook at the information desk.
2. Sign the patient's consent for medical treatment.
3. Wait in the waiting area for your queue number to be called.
4. Go to the Out-Patient Department clinic room for the consultation.

The document lists no requirement for a consultation.

## Vaccines (immunization services)

Free routine and campaign immunization to prevent vaccine-preventable diseases.

- **Who can apply:** Infants, children 0 to 4 years old, school-aged children, adolescents, pregnant women and senior citizens.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 29 minutes is the printed total. The step times listed add up to 34 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Immunization card, prenatal card or National Immunization Program card.** Where to get it: Municipal Health Office.

### Steps

1. Submit the immunization or prenatal card.
2. Sign the patient's consent for the immunization session.
3. Wait in the waiting area for your queue number to be called.
4. Receive the vaccine.

## Basic laboratory tests

Routine and screening tests: urinalysis, hemoglobin, blood typing, rapid tests for hepatitis B, syphilis, HIV and dengue NS1, and routine blood chemistry.

- **Who can apply:** Paombong patients with a laboratory request, and clients for screening.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 3 hours and 20 minutes for urinalysis, hemoglobin, blood typing and the rapid tests. 7 hours and 50 minutes for routine blood chemistry.

### What to bring

- **Laboratory request form.** Where to get it: Rural Health Unit.
- **Informed consent, for HIV and STI screening.** Where to get it: Rural Health Unit.

### Steps

1. Present the laboratory request form at the laboratory section.
2. Give your consent for HIV, syphilis or hepatitis B screening, if required.
3. Have the specimen collected.
4. Wait for the laboratory processing.
5. Claim the laboratory results.

## Dental services

Dental consultation, oral examination, tooth extraction, temporary filling and oral health education.

- **Who can apply:** Paombong residents who need dental care.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 1 hour and 25 minutes.

### What to bring

- **Dental record or patient card, if you are an existing patient.**

### Steps

1. Sign the patient's consent for the dental record.
2. Wait for your name to be called. It is first come, first served.
3. Go to the dental room for the consultation, examination and treatment.
4. Stay in the observation area until the observation period is over.
