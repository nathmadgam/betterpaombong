# Find a Health Center, Clinic or Hospital in Paombong

Government health centers and barangay health stations in Paombong, plus the private hospital, clinics and laboratory, as listed in the Department of Health's registry of health facilities.

---

## In an emergency

- **National emergency hotline:** 911
- **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office:** (044) 761-3199

## Rural health units and health centers

These are government health centers. The Municipal Health Officer, Jose Mari A. De Jesus, MD, holds office at Rural Health Unit I.

| Facility | Location | Telephone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paombong Rural Health Unit I | Poblacion | (044) 760-4041 |
| Paombong Rural Health Unit II | Pinagtulayan, San Isidro II | (044) 764-6101 |
| Paombong Super Health Center | Hangga, San Isidro II | Not listed |

## Barangay health stations

Twelve barangays have a barangay health station:

- Binakod
- Kapitangan, at Purok 4
- Malumot
- Masukol
- Pinalagdan, at Gumitna
- San Isidro I
- San Jose
- San Roque
- San Vicente
- Santa Cruz
- Santo Niño
- Santo Rosario, at Central

Poblacion and San Isidro II have none in the registry. The rural health units are in those two barangays.

## Hospital, dialysis and laboratory

The registry lists one hospital in Paombong, and it is private. It lists no government hospital in the town.

- **San Pascual Baylon Maternity and General Hospital:** private hospital, Level 1, 17 beds. Provincial Road, Santo Niño. Telephone (044) 665-2818.
- **Sacred Heart Hospital of Malolos, Inc.:** private dialysis clinic. 91 Unit 14 Cristia Bayan St, San Isidro I.
- **Excelgenics Diagnostic Laboratory:** private clinical laboratory. Poblacion.
