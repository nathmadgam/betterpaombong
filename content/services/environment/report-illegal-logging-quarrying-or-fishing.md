# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying, or Fishing

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the offices to ask are:

- **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO):** (044) 769-4569. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Municipal Agriculture Office:** (044) 760-4670. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

The MENRO handles tree cutting clearances, as its [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MENRO-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) shows. For fishing, the [Local Government Code](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/) (Section 482) gives the agriculture office the job of enforcing rules on agriculture and aquaculture.

Neither office has published how to report a violation, so please call or visit.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).
