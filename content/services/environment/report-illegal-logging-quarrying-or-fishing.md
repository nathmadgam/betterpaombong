# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying, or Fishing

Which offices to tell about illegal tree cutting or fishing, and how to get a clearance before cutting a tree.

---

## Where to go

- **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO):** (044) 769-4569. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Municipal Agriculture Office:** (044) 760-4670. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

For fishing, the [Local Government Code](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/) (Section 482) gives the agriculture office the job of enforcing rules on agriculture and aquaculture. Neither office has published how to report a violation, so please call or visit.

## Get a clearance before cutting a tree

The MENRO receives and assesses requests to cut a tree, and records the tree cutting clearance. You then prepare the documents for the tree clearance application at the CENRO, the community environment office of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources. The document says cutting is subject to conditions such as tree replacement or compensatory planting.

- **Who can apply:** Anyone who needs to cut a tree.
- **Fee:** None at the MENRO. The document does not say what the CENRO charges.
- **Processing time:** 11 minutes at the MENRO.

### What to bring

- **Certificate of No Objection (1 original and 1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The barangay captain of the area.
- **Any proof of land ownership (1 photocopy).** Where to get it: The landowner.
- **Sketch of the tree's location, or a picture (1 original).** You provide this.
- **Letter of intent addressed to the CENRO (1 original and 1 photocopy).** You write this.

### Steps

1. Go to the MENRO and sign the visitor logbook.
2. Say what you need.
3. Get the MENRO's list of requirements.
4. Prepare the documents needed for the tree clearance application at the CENRO.

The document prints the name of the office for the letter as "centro". This page reads it as the CENRO, which the steps name.
