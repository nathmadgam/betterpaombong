# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, or Fingerlings

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Municipal Agriculture Office:** (044) 760-4670. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

Under the [Local Government Code](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/) (Section 482), the municipal agriculture office is in charge of assistance and extension services for farmers and fisherfolk. The municipality's [Citizen's Charter page](https://paombong.gov.ph/citizens-charter/) has no document for this office yet, so this page cannot confirm the requirements or what is offered now. Please ask the office.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).
