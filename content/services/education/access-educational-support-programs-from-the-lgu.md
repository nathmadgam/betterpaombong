# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the offices to ask are:

- **Office of the Municipal Mayor:** (044) 794-0052. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Public Employment Service Office (PESO):** 0949-994-9587. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

The Mayor's office [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Mayor_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists scholarship applications for college students. The PESO's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CITIZENCHARTER_PESO_2025_final.pdf) lists the Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES) and career guidance for students.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).
