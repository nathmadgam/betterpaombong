# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU

Programs for students from the employment office (PESO): paid vacation work for students and career guidance seminars.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Mayor:** (044) 794-0052. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.
- **Public Employment Service Office (PESO):** 0949-994-9587. 2nd floor, Annex Building.

## Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES)

A Department of Labor and Employment program run by the PESO. It gives temporary work to poor but deserving students during summer or Christmas vacation, and to out-of-school youth and dependents of displaced or to-be-displaced workers during the year.

- **Who can apply:** Students aged 15 to 30 with a passing general weighted average. The parents' combined net income, including the student's own, must not exceed the regional poverty threshold. The document words this as "combined net income tax".
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 2 hours and 23 minutes.

### What to bring

- **SPES application form.** Where to get it: PESO.
- **Birth certificate.** Where to get it: Philippine Statistics Authority.
- **Previous and current grades.** Where to get it: Your school.
- **Income Tax Return.** Where to get it: Bureau of Internal Revenue.
- **Certificate of indigency.** Where to get it: The barangay where you live.
- **Valid ID of the student and of a parent.** You provide these.

### Steps

1. Register and submit your documents to the PESO, then wait for its advice on the interview schedule.
2. Fill out the SPES application form.
3. Submit the required documents and the accomplished application form.
4. Attend the SPES orientation.

## Career guidance seminar for students

The Career Development Support Program gives career guidance to students, with labor market information and current employment trends. The PESO arranges the seminar with a school.

- **Who can apply:** Grade 10 students.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 7 days, 4 hours and 40 minutes of preparation with the school, then 5 hours and 15 minutes for the seminar.

### Steps

1. The PESO and the school agree on the date and the number of students.
2. Students sign the attendance sheet and take part in the seminar.
3. Students answer the post-evaluation test and receive a Certificate of Attendance.

Students bring nothing. The PESO provides the letters, the attendance sheet, the certificates and the feedback form.
