# Apply for Local Scholarships

Paombong-specific details for this service are still being gathered by volunteers.

Until this page is complete, the office to ask is:

- **Office of the Municipal Mayor:** (044) 794-0052. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

The office's [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Mayor_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf) lists the processing of scholarship applications for college students. The document gives the requirements and steps.

For every other office, see [municipal offices and contact numbers](../../government/departments/municipal-offices).

Know how this works in Paombong? [Help write this page](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong).

_Sources: [Municipality of Paombong, Contact Us page](https://paombong.gov.ph/contact-us/), [Citizen's Charter of the Office of the Municipal Mayor](https://paombong.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Mayor_s-Office-Citizen_s-Charter.pdf). The Citizen's Charter was read on 24 November 2026. Spotted an error? [Open an issue](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong/issues)._
