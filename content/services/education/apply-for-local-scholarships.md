# Apply for Local Scholarships

How college students apply for the municipal scholarship at the Office of the Municipal Mayor.

---

## Where to go

- **Office of the Municipal Mayor:** (044) 794-0052. 2nd floor, Main Building, Municipal Hall.

## Apply for the municipal scholarship

Financial assistance for qualified and deserving students. Applicants are evaluated against the eligibility requirements and the available funds.

- **Who can apply:** College students.
- **Fee:** None.
- **Processing time:** 5 days and 10 minutes is the printed total. The step times listed add up to about 7 days.

### What to bring

- **Letter of application.** You write this.
- **Personal Data Sheet.** You fill this out.
- **Barangay certificate of indigency.** Where to get it: Your barangay hall.
- **Certificate of enrollment or registration.** Where to get it: Your school registrar's office.
- **Certificate of grades or latest report card.** Where to get it: Your school registrar's office.
- **Photocopy of your school ID or any valid ID.** You provide this.

### Steps

1. Submit all the required documents to the Office of the Municipal Mayor.
2. Wait for the evaluation.
3. Claim the financial assistance on the date set for its release.
