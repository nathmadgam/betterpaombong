# About Better Paombong

Better Paombong is a volunteer-run portal that brings public information about the Municipal Government of Paombong, Bulacan into one place.

---

## Not an official government website

Better Paombong is not run by, funded by or endorsed by the Municipality of Paombong. For official announcements and transactions, go to the municipal government itself:

- **Official website:** [paombong.gov.ph](https://paombong.gov.ph/)
- **Offices and phone numbers:** [municipal offices and contact numbers](government/departments/municipal-offices)

## What you can find here

- **Officials:** the Mayor, the Vice Mayor and the Sangguniang Bayan for the 2025 to 2028 term.
- **Offices:** who heads each municipal office, where it is and the number to call.
- **Services:** which office to go to for health care, schools, permits and other municipal services.

Many service pages are still short. They name the office to ask and link to the municipality's own documents. Volunteers add details over time.

## Where the information comes from

Facts about officials, offices, phone numbers and fees are taken from public sources. Each page links to its sources at the bottom. The main ones are:

- the [Municipality of Paombong website](https://paombong.gov.ph/), including its [Citizen's Charter](https://paombong.gov.ph/citizens-charter/)
- the [Provincial Government of Bulacan](https://bulacan.gov.ph/cities-and-municipalities/paombong/)
- the Department of Health's [National Health Facility Registry](https://nhfr.doh.gov.ph/)
- the Department of Education's [machine-ready files](https://www.deped.gov.ph/machine-ready-files/)

Details can change after a page is written. Before you travel or pay a fee, confirm with the office.

## Who makes it

Better Paombong is maintained by volunteers. It is part of the [BetterGov.ph](https://about.bettergov.ph/) community, a volunteer civic technology movement, and it is built on that community's open-source template for local government portals.

## Contact

- **Report a mistake or suggest a page:** [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong/issues)
- **Help write a page:** see the [project on GitHub](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong)

The volunteers cannot process permits, payments or complaints. For those, contact the municipal office that handles the service.
