# Accessibility

We want everyone in Paombong to be able to use this portal, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard or a small phone.

---

## Where things stand

Better Paombong has not had a formal accessibility audit. We cannot yet say that it meets the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).

What the portal does today:

- Information is published as text with headings, lists and tables, not as pictures of text.
- Pages adjust to phone screens. On a small screen, a table can be switched between a table view and a list view.

## Known limits

- Page content is in English only.
- Requirements, fees and forms are often PDF files on the municipality's website. We link to them, and we cannot change how accessible those files are.
- Some service pages are still short and only name the office to ask.

## Tell us about a problem

If something on this portal is hard to read or use, please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/nathmadgam/betterpaombong/issues). It helps to say which page it was, what you were trying to do and which device or assistive technology you use.

Volunteers maintain this portal in their free time, so a fix may take a while.
